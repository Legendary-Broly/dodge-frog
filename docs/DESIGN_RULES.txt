# Design Rules

## Core Idea

A third-person physics comedy race where tiny players cross increasingly hostile highways while cars, items, bodies, and other players create chaos.

## Core Rule

Complexity comes from players interacting with simple rules, never from the rules themselves.

## Movement

- Walk = precision.
- Sprint = speed with fixed stamina.
- Jump = vertical correction only. No extra forward speed.
- Dive = full commitment and full ragdoll recovery.

## Player Physics

- Walking into another player = cosmetic bump only.
- Sprinting into another player = both players stumble.
- Sprint-jumping into another player = both players stumble.
- Diving = full ragdoll.
- Diving into another player = both players full ragdoll.

## Cars

- Car hit = instant death.
- Dead player body remains in the road as a permanent ragdoll physics object.
- Dead bodies keep nameplates and only change cosmetically (like X eyes).
- Dead bodies can be hit by cars, items, other bodies, and players.

## Items

- One item slot.
- Walk through item box to receive a random item.
- One button uses held item.
- No inventory.
- No HP.
- No damage numbers.
- No status effects.
- Items create movement, hazards, or physics interactions.

## Rounds

- First player across wins.
- After first place finishes, a countdown begins.
- Players score by placement.
- Session leader wears the crown next round.
